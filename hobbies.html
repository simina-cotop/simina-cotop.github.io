Cinema & Film Studies

Cinema has been a profound passion - with over 1600 films in my collection, spanning from classic cinema to modern masterpieces. Each film offers unique insights into storytelling techniques, cinematography, and the art of character development. I enjoy talking about movies, watching them and learning about how they are made and about the crew involved in making them. I combine my love for movies with the one for lists and I usually watch movies which I find on one list or another, or choose one of my favorite actors and directors and binge-watch their movies. On my Letterboxd account you can find my all my movies and my movie lists.
